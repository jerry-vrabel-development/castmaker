## System Reference Document 5.1

The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

Castmaker is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast. All character names are original to Castmaker. Only SRD ancestries (Human, High Elf, Hill Dwarf, Lightfoot Halfling, Rock Gnome, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Tiefling, Dragonborn) and the twelve SRD classes are used.

## U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics

Job counts for modern occupations come from the BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, "Number of Jobs, 2025" (https://www.bls.gov/ooh/), retrieved October 2026. BLS data is a U.S. government work in the public domain. Castmaker is not endorsed by BLS.

## Gallup

Orientation odds in "Real" mode use figures from Gallup's survey of 13,454 U.S. adults conducted in 2025 and published February 16, 2026 (https://news.gallup.com/poll/702206/lgbtq-identification-holds.aspx). The 1% asexual figure is an estimate; Gallup does not report it separately. Castmaker is not endorsed by Gallup.
